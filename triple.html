Triple Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid with 1, 2 and 3. Each number must appear exactly three times in every row, column and outlined 3×3 box.

Go to clue-count simulation ↓

Generating a uniquely solvable puzzle…

Choose a number, then tap a blank cell. Or focus a cell and type 1–3; use Backspace to erase. Notes are optional pencil marks. Red cells mean a number appears more than three times in a row, column or box.

Clue-count simulation

Generate puzzles and remove clues one at a time until every remaining clue is necessary for a unique solution. There is no search limit.

This is the observed distribution for this generator and random digging order, not a uniform sample of all Triple Sudoku puzzles. Each run starts a fresh sample and leaves your playable puzzle untouched.
