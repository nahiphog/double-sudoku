Triple Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid with 1, 2 and 3. Each number must appear exactly three times in every row, column and outlined 3×3 box.

Generating a uniquely solvable puzzle…

Choose a number, then tap a blank cell. Or focus a cell and type 1–3; use Backspace to erase. Notes are optional pencil marks. Red cells mean a number appears more than three times in a row, column or box.

Solution walkthrough

Work through the current puzzle from its original clues. This separate board leaves your entries and notes unchanged.

Uses completed digit counts, singles, two or three forced copies, subsets based on remaining copies, locked candidates, fish and short contradiction chains. A puzzle may need deductions beyond these techniques; the walkthrough will say when it is stuck.

Small digits are candidates. Yellow marks supporting cells; blue outlines mark affected cells. Red struck-through candidates will be removed, and green candidates will be placed. Each digit is allowed three times per unit.
